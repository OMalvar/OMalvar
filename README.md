## 🧬 About Me

I am a researcher at **CSIC (Consejo Superior de Investigaciones Científicas)**, Spain, working at the intersection of **science and engineering**. My work combines **microfluidics**, **nanomechanics**, **microbiology**, and advanced experimental techniques to develop novel biosensing and characterization tools at the micro- and nanoscale.

I am passionate about bridging fundamental science and engineering to tackle real-world challenges — from understanding bacterial mechanics to exploring emerging therapies.

I also actively contribute to the scientific community as a **peer reviewer** for leading journals, including:

- 📖 *Microfluidics and Nanofluidics* (Springer)
- 📖 *ACS Sensors* (American Chemical Society)

## 🔬 Research Areas & Scientific Interests

| Area | Details |
|------|---------|
| 🦠 **Microbiology** | *E. coli* MC1061 culture experiments in LB and minimal media; bacterial growth and morphology studies |
| 💊 **Antibiotic Assays** | Amikacin exposure experiments; morphological analysis using **Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM)** |
| ⚖️ **Nanomechanics** | Nanomechanical mass measurements using resonant micro/nanosystems |
| 🏭 **Microfabrication** | Cleanroom processes, AZ ECI 3027 photolithography, **RIE on SiN**, MEMS/NEMS fabrication |
| 💧 **Microfluidics & Biosensing** | Microfluidic chip design and integration for lab-on-a-chip biosensing applications |
| 🧪 **Cell & Gene Therapy** | Interest in **lentivirus** and **CAR-T cell therapy** — exploring micro/nanotechnologies for next-generation therapeutics |
